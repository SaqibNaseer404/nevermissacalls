About Never Miss Calls

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Never Miss Calls (nevermissacalls.com) is run by Saqib Naseer Khan. It gives home-service businesses — plumbers, HVAC, roofers, electricians, garage door teams — an instant text-back system for the calls they can't pick up.

The problem

You're on a job, under a sink or up a ladder, and a new customer's call hits voicemail. Most of those callers never leave a message — they just call the next business on Google. Every missed call is a job that went to a competitor.

How it works

  • A call you can't answer gets an automatic text in under a minute.
  • The text asks what's wrong and where the job is, and keeps the conversation going.
  • It books the job straight onto your calendar — you stay on the tools, your number doesn't change.
