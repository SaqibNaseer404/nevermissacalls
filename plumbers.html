01 — How it works

The questions a good dispatcher asks.

The second a call is missed, the caller gets a text. Then the conversation qualifies the job:

  • What's the problem — leak, blockage, water heater, no water?
  • Is water still actively leaking? If yes, it flags urgent and can route straight to you.
  • Where's the job — inside your service area?
  • Booked into your real calendar — or handed to you when you'd rather take it personally.

You set the rules: your prices, your area, your hours. Nothing books outside them.

06 — Straight answers

Plumber questions, handled.

What happens with a 2am emergency call?

The text-back fires at 2am the same as 2pm — it qualifies the job, and anything you've marked urgent (active leak, no water, flooding) routes straight to you by your rules. You sleep through the tire-kickers; the real emergencies wake you.

Will it quote my prices to customers?

Only the prices and rules you set. Most plumbers have it book the visit and leave quoting to them — the flow does exactly what you tell it, nothing more.

Do I have to change my phone number?

No. The system sits behind your existing number and only acts when a call is missed. Your number stays yours — and if you ever leave, your number and conversations go with you.

Will customers know it's automated?

It texts like a dispatcher — short and natural. Most customers don't realise until they're booked, and by then the job is yours.

Can I take over a conversation?

Anytime. Jump in from your phone and the system steps aside.
