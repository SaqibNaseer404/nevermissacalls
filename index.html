09 — Straight answers

Objections, handled.

Do we need to change our phone number?

No. The workflow sits behind your existing business number and responds when a call is missed.

Will it sound like a robot?

No. It texts like a dispatcher — short, natural, and focused on the job. Customers generally don't realize it's automated until they're booked.

Is this a voice bot?

No. The starting offer is missed-call text-back and booking — deliberately simpler and less intrusive than making every caller talk to a robot.

Can a customer reach a real person?

Yes. Your team can take over any conversation, and urgent or complex jobs route straight to a human on your rules.

Will it promise times we can't honor?

No. Availability and booking rules are defined with you. It offers approved windows, requests a callback, or hands off — never invents.

We are a barber shop / salon, not a big home-service company. Is this for us?

Yes — if you live on bookings. The system books the chair with the right barber or stylist, and sends reminders that cut no-shows. Your ticket is smaller, so for appointment shops the pilot is built around filling chairs and keeping regulars, not one big job.

I run a clinic — what about patient privacy?

The text-back never asks for medical details, symptoms, or records — only a name, the kind of appointment, and a time. Anything medical goes to your team on a call, exactly the way it does today. Booking basics by text; healthcare stays in the room.

What about other local businesses?

If customers call to book and a missed call means they go elsewhere — auto repair, cleaning, landscaping — the same system fits. The questions and booking rules are built for your trade.

Why is the audit free? What's the catch?

No catch. The audit is a 15-minute call where we count your missed calls and price what they cost you. If the number is small, we will tell you not to buy. If it is big, the pilot sells itself — that's the whole strategy.

What do I pay after the pilot, exactly?

$500/month for the full system — text-back, website chat, booking, reminders, and support. Your $500 pilot fee is credited in full, so you never pay it twice. Every price is in the pricing section above; there is no second price list on a sales call.
