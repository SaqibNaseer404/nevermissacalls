Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Never Miss Calls (nevermissacalls.com) is run by Saqib Naseer Khan. This page explains what data we collect and why, in plain language.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site — which pages are viewed and roughly where visitors come from. Google Analytics sets cookies in your browser and may collect your IP address, device and browser information. We use this only to improve the site. You can opt out with a browser extension like Google's own opt-out add-on.

Booking a call

When you book an intro call, our scheduling provider (Cal.com) collects the details you enter — your name, email address, and anything you write in the booking notes — so the meeting can happen. Cal.com has its own privacy policy governing that data.

Contacting us

If you email hello@nevermissacalls.com, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and we use it only to reply to you. We never sell your information to anyone, and we never share it except where the law requires it.

If you call or text a business that uses Never Miss Calls

Many local businesses use Never Miss Calls to answer customers they would otherwise miss. This section is for you if you have called or texted one of those businesses.

What gets processed. When a business misses your call, our system sends you a text message so the conversation can continue. To do that, the system processes:

  • Your phone number, and the date and time of your call
  • The text conversation between you and the business
  • The details you choose to share in that conversation — typically your name, the job you need done, and the time that suits you

Why. This information is used for one purpose only: so the business can respond to you, understand the job, and book it in. It is not used for advertising, and it is never sold to anyone.

Who handles it. The business you contacted is in charge of your details. Never Miss Calls and the phone, messaging, and AI providers that run the system process the information only on the business's behalf, so the system works.

How to opt out. Reply STOP to any text from the system and you will not be texted again. You can also ask the business directly to remove your details, and they can do that at any time.

What we never collect. The system never asks for payment card details by text. If a business is a clinic, the system never asks for medical details, symptoms, or records — only a name, the kind of appointment, and a time.

How long it is kept. Your conversation and contact details are kept while the business uses the service, so the business can follow up with you. If you ask for your details to be removed, or if a business stops using the service, that data is deleted within 30 days. The business owner controls their leads and can ask us for an export or a full deletion at any time.

Data retention

Analytics data is kept according to Google's retention settings. Emails you send us are kept as long as needed to handle your request.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with an updated date.
